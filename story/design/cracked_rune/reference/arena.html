Elements react
  • Your role decides what every pair becomes. Fire + Water is Steam Detonation for a Destroyer, Scalding Fog for a Controller, Warm Mist for a Guardian, Mirage Vapor for a Trickster and Mana Supernova for an Arcanist.
  • Wet targets take double lightning and freeze instantly under ice. Casting lightning while wet shocks you.
  • Fire dries and thaws; water and ice put fires out. Opposites (fire/ice, light/dark) no longer cancel — your role turns the clash into its own spell.
  • Earth shatters frozen enemies for double damage; fire on ice is a Thermal Shock crit.
  • Nature roots — on wet ground it entangles. Fire on roots burns hotter. Wind fans flames to the next enemy and chills the wet.
  • Light blinds and purges curses for ×1.5; dark curses (+25% damage taken) and drains life to you. Light and dark cancel.
  • Water + Ice draws a piercing Ice Spear that freezes what it runs through. Three different elements hit a tier harder than two.
  • Lightning charges puddles, ice freezes them, fire lights dry brush. Rune barrels explode.
  • Bulwarks block bolts from the front — flank them, or strike while the maul is raised.
